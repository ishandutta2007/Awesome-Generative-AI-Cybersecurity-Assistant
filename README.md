# Awesome-Gaming-Headset-Hardware

**Curated List of Commercial Headsets & Open-Source GitHub Projects**  
*Focused on Audio Quality, Latency, Comfort & Open-Source Control Software*  
**Last updated: October 2026**

This repository tracks notable **commercial gaming headsets** and **open-source projects** that maximize their potential. These tools help gamers choose the right headset for competitive play, immersive single-player experiences, and long-session comfort—and unlock advanced features with community-driven firmware and control software.

**Examples** include Xbox Wireless Headset, SteelSeries Arctis Nova Pro, HyperX Cloud II, Razer BlackShark V2, Astro A50, Corsair Virtuoso, Logitech G PRO X, Turtle Beach Stealth 700, EPOS H6PRO, and Beyerdynamic MMX 300 (the category leaders).

**Open-source emphasis**: The open-source headset ecosystem is **mature and production-proven**. **HeadsetControl** is the cross-platform standard for controlling USB gaming headsets on Linux, macOS, and Windows, supporting sidetone, battery status, LED control, and equalizers . **Linux-Arctis-Manager** replaces SteelSeries GG on Linux with ChatMix, 10-band/15-band EQ via PulseAudio LADSPA, and microphone noise suppression . **OpenPineBuds** provides community-maintained open firmware for the PineBuds Pro—the only wireless earbuds with fully open-source firmware . **EasyEffects** delivers PipeWire-native audio processing with parametric EQ, limiter, and compressor . **AutoEq** generates correction profiles for 10,000+ headphone models .

| **[HeadsetControl](https://github.com/Sapd/HeadsetControl)** — **The cross-platform standard for controlling USB gaming headsets.** **C++20 rewrite** (v4.0.0, July 2026) . Supports **Linux, macOS, and Windows** . **Features**: Sidetone adjustment, battery status, LED control, inactive time setting, and **equalizer control** . **Supported headsets**: SteelSeries Arctis 7/PRO 2019, Corsair VOID (Pro/Elite), Logitech G930/G533/G633/G933, and many more . **Library usage** available for C++, C, Python, and Rust . **GPL-2.0** . | [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/Sapd/HeadsetControl?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/Sapd/HeadsetControl/stargazers) | ~2,000 |

## ⚠️ Disclaimer

- This is a **community-curated** list — not exhaustive and not an endorsement.
- Gaming headsets are **commercial hardware**; open-source software can extend functionality but may void warranties and carries risk of bricking devices.
- **Open-source reality**: The open-source ecosystem for gaming headsets is **mature and production-proven**. **HeadsetControl** is the cross-platform standard for controlling USB gaming headsets on Linux, macOS, and Windows . **Linux-Arctis-Manager** replaces SteelSeries GG on Linux with ChatMix, 10-band/15-band EQ, and microphone noise suppression . **OpenPineBuds** provides community-maintained open firmware for the PineBuds Pro—the only wireless earbuds with fully open-source firmware . **EasyEffects** delivers PipeWire-native audio processing . **AutoEq** generates correction profiles for 10,000+ headphone models . However, **commercial headsets** (SteelSeries Arctis Nova Pro, Astro A50, Corsair Virtuoso, Logitech G PRO X) provide **polished hardware engineering, premium build quality, and dedicated support** that open-source alternatives may lack. The open-source path is **genuinely viable** for Linux users, audio enthusiasts, and anyone willing to trade polish for full control.
- **Pricing caveat**: All pricing figures are **verified against cited search results** but may change without notice. **SteelSeries Arctis Nova Pro ranges from $199.99–$374.99** depending on platform and wireless/wired configuration . **Astro A50 Gen 5 is $299.99** , **A50 X is $749.95** . **Turtle Beach Stealth 700 Gen 3 is $199.99** . **Xbox Wireless Headset refreshed model is $109.99** . **EPOS H6PRO is $111–$178** . Always check the manufacturer's official page for current pricing.
